# Example: AI Coding Judgment Draft

## Prompt

```text
/cogx-draft
我想写一篇关于 AI 编程助手改变工程师判断力的文章，但不想写成工具测评。
```

## Expected Shape

- States a clear thesis about engineering judgment rather than tool features.
- Produces an editable article draft, not only an outline.
- Includes counterarguments and places where personal examples or data are needed.
- Marks facts, citations, and anecdotes that need verification.
- Avoids generic productivity claims and AI-flavored filler.
